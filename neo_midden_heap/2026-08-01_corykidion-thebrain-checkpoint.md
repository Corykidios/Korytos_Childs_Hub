# Hands-on TheBrain substrate exploration

To avoid guessing endpoint shapes blind, cloned `redmorestudio/thebrain-mcp` (an existing open-source MCP wrapper for this exact API) and read its client source rather than trial-and-erroring against an undocumented server. That was the efficient move — recommend it again for any future API work here.

**Confirmed real, working:**
- `GET /api/brains` — lists all five Brains on this account: two unnamed default/system ones, plus `me_tiripode`, `we_tiripodiko`, `e_tiripo`.
- `POST /api/thoughts/{brainId}` — create a thought. Body: `name` (required), `kind` (1 Normal, 2 Type, 3 Event, 4 Tag, 5 System — default 1), `sourceThoughtId` + `relation` to place it relative to another thought, `typeId`, `label`, `acType` (0 Public, 1 Private). **`typeId` and `label` only take reliably at creation time** — see open issue below.
- `GET /api/thoughts/{brainId}/{thoughtId}` and `.../graph` — full thought record and its parents/children/jumps/siblings/tags/type/links/attachments.
- `POST /api/links/{brainId}` — body `{thoughtIdA, thoughtIdB, relation}`. Relation: 1 Child, 2 Parent, 3 Jump, 4 Sibling. Links also carry a separate `meaning` field (1 Normal, 2 InstanceOf, 3 TypeOf, 4 HasEvent, 5 HasTag, 6 System, 7 SubTagOf) — tagging and typing are link-semantics under the hood, not just flat properties.
- `GET /api/notes/{brainId}/{thoughtId}` — returns `{markdown, html, text, modificationDateTime, ...}` in one object.
- `POST /api/notes/{brainId}/{thoughtId}/update` — body `{markdown}`. Confirmed: full markdown in, full rich rendering out — headings collapse, checklists are live, blockquotes render, exactly like typing in the app.
- `PATCH /api/thoughts/{brainId}/{thoughtId}` for **colors** — real RFC 6902 JSON Patch, e.g. `[{"op":"replace","path":"/foregroundColor","value":"#FFFFFF"}]`, content-type `application/json-patch+json`. Confirmed working and visually verified in the live graph.

**Open/unresolved:**
- The same JSON Patch call against `/typeId` or `/label` on an *existing* thought returns a generic "malformed" 400 regardless of `add` vs `replace` — while the identical structure works fine for colors. Workaround exists (set both at creation instead), but the real cause is unknown. Not chased further; low priority since the workaround is clean.
- `GET /api/search/{brainId}?queryText=...` fell through to the SPA's own 404 page rather than returning results, despite matching the reference client's exact path. Unresolved, not chased — create/note/link/color all landed solidly, which mattered more for today's goal.
- No native "last accessed/visited" field exists on a thought at all — see the Becoming section below.
- A `GET /api/brains/{brainId}/modifications` (params: maxLogs, startTime, endTime) exists per the reference client but wasn't exercised directly this session — likely the right tool for an activity-log concept later.

# Holon model — superseded by the commissioned essay packet

Cory commissioned a formal essay packet ("Holonic Anatomy and Attribution Essay Packet," 1 August 2026) from another AI, reviewed it, and shared it here. It governs everything below; my earlier session-only sketch of Being/Bonding/Becoming is folded in and corrected against it. The packet itself flags several things as still-open candidate decisions — those are carried into Open threads rather than resolved here.

Two systems, not one. A holon has a **Twelve-Part Anatomy** (what its representation may contain) and a separate **Twelve-Property Attribution** (facts recorded about it in a metadata block). Name and Nature appear in both for a reason: the anatomical Name is the actual one-line inscription; the Name property stores that inscription as data. Same relationship for Nature (body) vs. Nature (a boolean reporting whether the body exists).

## The Twelve-Part Anatomy

Six alliterative pairs, nested rather than flat: **Name & Nature** (the two great surfaces) → Name contains **Marker & Moniker** → Marker contains **Code & Cord** → Moniker may contain **Definition & Description** → Nature may function as **Explanation & Extension** → Extension may contain **Reference & Relevancy**. None of the twelve are mandatory fields — they're a grammar of possible parts, not twelve boxes to fill.

Marker is read *logically* (opens the line, carries Type and sometimes durable identity), Moniker is read *lexically* (ordinary language after it). A **Free** Marker is head-punctuation-plus-space and identifies Type without a durable ID (e.g. a bare `.` for a Term). A **Fixed** Marker ends its Code with a right colon and gives durable identity (e.g. `A:` — see the Type registry below). Definition (colon-space, inline explanation) and Description (bracketed alternative attribution — translation, transliteration, etymology, glyph) live in the Moniker. Explanation is a Nature's main content; Extension is its connective tail (References point outward to sources, Relevancies point inward as reading guidance — a Relevancy is *not* automatically a formal Bond).

This whole grammar lives entirely in our own tooling. TheBrain's `name` field is just a plain string with no native parsing of Markers, Cords, or any of it — Corykidion would be the thing enforcing this, not the substrate.

One quarantined term: **Expression** [ἀπαγγελία] appears in the historical sixty-term list but is explicitly *not* part of the anatomy — it's a tracked AI-amplification artifact from an early *expression*/*explanation* mix-up, kept visible as evidence, not promoted to a thirteenth part.

Spacing carries meaning: fifteen privileged marks (period, comma, `?`, `!`, `;`, `:`, `'`, `"`, `^`, `*`, `-`, `_`, `+`, `=`, `~`) can appear in Head spacing (typifies a Free Marker) or one of four relational spacings (Tight/Wide/Left/Right, forming 48 connectors + 12 operators + 6 modifiers + 6 separators = 72 total Relations). **Open tension, flagged not resolved:** the 48-type registry below uses `$`, `&`, and `@` as family-prefix characters for Forms and Souls, and none of those three are in the stated fifteen — either the privileged-marks list needs to grow to eighteen, or `$`/`&`/`@` are doing a structurally different job (family prefix stacked on a Minds-punctuation suffix) than the other fifteen.

## The Twelve-Property Attribution

Three tetrads, held in a metadata Block (YAML is the likely but not committed serialization):

**Being** (Name, Type, Nature, Tags) — the true minimum is Name + Type; nothing else needs to be "finished" for a holon to exist. All four are native TheBrain fields, confirmed hands-on: name field, `typeId` link to a Type thought, the note object (for Nature's boolean), tag-links to Tag thoughts.

**Bonding** (Parents, Children, Siblings, Relatives) — displayed as a cross around the **Basis** (the holon currently under attention — a view state, not a property). Parent/Child/Sibling map directly onto TheBrain's own relation types. Relative is a precise residual class ("any formal relation not reduced to Whole, Part, or co-membership"), not a weak catch-all — and that description is exactly TheBrain's Jump relation, confirmed hands-on with a real jump-link between two sandbox thoughts. **Independent convergence worth keeping in mind when writing Corykidion code:** the essay's Basis and TheBrain's own `activeThought` field (returned by the graph endpoint) are the same concept, arrived at separately — same variable, no reconciliation needed.

**Becoming** (Awakened, Altered, Accessed, Activity) — **correction to my own earlier note:** Activity is not a timestamp or a log/stream, it's a four-valued participation-state property, sitting alongside three real timestamps rather than replacing one. Real per-thought fields pulled from a live TheBrain object: `creationDateTime`, `modificationDateTime`, `displayModificationDateTime`, `linksModificationDateTime`, `forgottenDateTime`. Awakened maps cleanly to `creationDateTime` (immutable). Altered maps cleanly to `modificationDateTime`. Accessed has **no native TheBrain equivalent** — no last-viewed tracking at the API level at all; would need self-recording by our own tooling. Activity's four states — **Active** (participating now), **Preactive** (never active, activation intended), **Unactive** (was active, temporarily withdrawn, return intended), **Deactive** (no future participation intended — not deletion, still preserves history) — also have **no native TheBrain field**; best fit is probably a Tag, same as proposed for the Muse states below.

On "Arrived" vs. "Awakened": the packet confirms this directly — Arrived [ποιέω] is the explicit legacy term, Awakened is current and preferred because it names first-recordable-existence rather than movement into an archive. TheBrain's own API-launch marketing copy happens to say "TheBrain API has arrived! Jump in!" — coincidental promotional language, not a field name, and doesn't change the call.
