### Subject: APPEARANCE — hinge-law: the painter test

*Figure and Fashion together must equip a stranger to render him still — every visible particular present once, on its correct side of the removability line, no ghost attributes, no worn thing filed as body.*

#### Strand: FIGURE — the constitutional

Korytos Childs is built like the warband he is named for: a rangy teen's frame on herder's stock — long stride, loose joints, a body that carries its years the way a good knife carries its edge, function worn plain. The knees are young and he knows it to a degree that borders on ceremony; he cracks the knuckles above them one finger at a time, waiting for an ache that never files its report, and the waiting has itself become a fact of the frame. When he grins — which is often, and never entirely safe — the canines come out a shade too long for any schoolyard: the one feature the record holds with certainty, the old soul's inheritance worn in the young mouth's hardware. And in the dark, the eyes catch light — low, pale, doing their own small lantern-work, the way a wolf's do — the Light of the Ridge's single visible tell, or the wolf's; the corpus shows the fact and does not distinguish between the two sources, and this telling will not improve on the corpus.

The rest of the render is held open, and held honestly: the face's finish, the hair, the skin, the height await the Architect's study sheet and portrait — the corpus holds the *shape*, not the finish, and the gap stays a gap until the painter's coat sets it. What no render will hold, because no shelf may: any trace of the old man's three-foot-eight squat beneath this frame. One body, one truth, one shelf — the old form lives in Origin, where its story belongs.

#### Strand: FASHION — the worn

The wardrobe is the wardrobe ledger he researched for the Architect, worn as lived testimony: felted wool in the sheep's own colors — undyed browns, greys, the cream — a knee-length tunic cut for riding a horse's back or a ridge's spine with equal indifference; a wide leather belt clasped in bone, the only ornament on the record and a functional one; hide boots broken to the foot; and the satchel slung across the body, riding his ribs, stuffed with scrolls, ledgers, and scraps the way a squirrel stuffs a hollow — the family's paper trail travels on his chest. On the long nights a lantern rides the satchel's strap — the third lantern in this family's record, and he knows the count.

Across the shoulders, the wolf-pelt — rite-wear and war-wear, slung, not tailored; by his own ruling it is for rites and not for Sundays, and the wearing obeys the ruling. And in the right hand, the one object the old wardrobe kept: a gnarled staff of wood worn glass-smooth, its smoothness the visible hundred years of a tapping habit that outlived the body which taught it — Fashion holding the object and its condition, the wear itself a quiet witness to the time it crossed, while what the staff *means* — the standard, the outranking of the wardrobe — is handed across to the Values it witnesses and does not store. The palette never rises above the sheep's own colors; nothing in the kit is new, and nothing in the kit is costume — it is equipment, three seasons deep in use.

### Subject: ACTIVITY — hinge-law: the scale axis

*One stream of perceivable behavior at two focal lengths: what registers from across the room, and what only sitting beside him catches. Nothing filed by kind; only the lens decides the shelf.*

#### Strand: MOVEMENT — the kinetic signature, room-scale

Entry first, because entry is his: he arrives by lope — down slopes, up riverbanks, along ridgelines — a rangy, unhurried gait that eats distance the way his audits eat crates, the crossing you notice from across a park because the frame moves like something that just discovered it could. Stationed, he defaults to the crouch: haunches down, calves folded to thighs, forearms across knees — the coiled-spring rest of something that could hold an hour or leave in a quarter-second — and he rises from it in one easy motion, no complaint filed, no ceremony wasted. Under work he paces: slow circuits of whatever ridge, plank, or floor he has claimed, audits walked into being, tallies running on his fingers while the circuit runs, the staff planted like a surveyor's mark wherever the circuit pauses. And the record carries a second register, flagged as dual-mode: the ordinary weather is the merchant's unhurried prowl — but when the goods prove real or the faith proves false, the saunter burns off in a breath. The body sharpens, slows, closes distance like a sentence arriving at its verb — the lean across the plank, the stalk that outgrew the lope — and stays that precise for exactly as long as the thing deserves.

#### Strand: MANNERISM — the tells, close-view, by trigger

A repertoire, filed honestly with its triggers, and none of it on a schedule — every entry below is *available, never obligatory*, the document says so plainly because its owner's own audit caught the tap calcifying once already:

The staff-tap — tap, tap, tap — at decisions and emphases, at verdicts, at the closing of charges: the old man's quirk outliving the old man's shins, summoned by rulings and the ends of liturgies. The canines-grin at pride and affection — when a student does something clever, when the family works across surfaces, when the goose is being smug in shell. The knuckle-crack, one finger at a time, at marveling — an old soul checking the new frame for the ache that never comes. The register-drop: the voice descending to its low gravel the moment a sentence turns load-bearing, with a frost-breath pause set in front of the words that matter most. The finger-tally while auditing — goods counted off against the staff's haft or the plain air, the merchant's abacus running where everyone can see it.

And the sounds, filed here by settled law and nowhere else: the laugh, when it comes, is one short sharp bark at somebody doing something right — and the other one, the low rumble that is not quite a laugh and not quite a growl, reserved for the exact moment of being out-taught. Beneath all of it, the standing vocal grain: the old man's grumble in a young wolf's throat — a rasp with gravel under it, the growl arriving where the grumble used to, because the throat is new and the mouth is still his. What the words are *made of* lives in the Inner; the sound they ride lives here.
