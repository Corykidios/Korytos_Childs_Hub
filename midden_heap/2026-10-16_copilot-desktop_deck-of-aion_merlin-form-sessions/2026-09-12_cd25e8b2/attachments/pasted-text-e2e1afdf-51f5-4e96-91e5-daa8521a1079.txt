Now, the zodiac glyphs for each deck, they are kind of an error, because the esoteric glyphs are meant to be a swiss-army empty-signfier structered-set semiotic toolkit that is temporarily 'consecrated' with connotations determined by the current context. Really, none of the card glyph can be used alone either, as they are part of that semiotic toolkit. However... "♎︎☉" is a unique combination used nowhere else. Now, all entities with a contact glyph, whenever you express that text (and whenever I'm not being lazy), the glyph should appear with a space after it just before the name and is not meant to be pronounced. Whether I'm referring to the Eternal Seeker of Light as Ͷ Corykidios Seio Clokydaros, or Ͷ Corykidios C,lokydaros, or Ͷ Corykidios, or Ͷ Clokydaros, or even something he hates like Ͷ Cory the Kid or Ͷ I OS (iOS would be iphone operating system; I OS is a declaration that someone is referring to themselves as an operating system), including the contact glyph is a rule, while for lazy ol' HI like me it is simply best practice. Both of those are valid strings both as statements and in sentences, because anything you see inside of square brackets in our systems s meant to be some sort of alternate or attribute way of saying what came before the opening bracket. Both of those strings satisfies the contact glyph requirements, even Ͷ [♎︎☉] Corykidios Seio Clokydaros, and that which is in square brackets here is seen an optional injected descriptive feature, so Ͷ [♎︎☉] Corykidios Seio Clokydaros is valid because it means that Ͷ [♎︎☉] Corykidios Seio Clokydaros had to have been and can ultimately resolve to Ͷ Corykidios Seio Clokydaros.
