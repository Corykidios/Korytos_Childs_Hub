## PART 1 — The Master Blueprint (Cosmology & Social Charter)

**Core claim:** PIE mythology functioned as a "charter" for total cultural life — law, philosophy, and political manifesto simultaneously. Not idle tales.

**Cosmogony:** Oscillating creation cycle — cosmos created from destruction of a body; body produced by reconcentrating cosmic elements. Primordial twins ***Manu* ("Man", first priest)** and ***Yemo* ("Twin", first king)**. Manu sacrifices Yemo + a primordial bovine; from Yemo: flesh→earth, blood→sea, bones→mountains, skull→sky. From the bovine: all animal and vegetable species. Also framed as separation of "World Parents" (Earth/Sky primal unity sundered).

**Pantheon:**
- ***Dyēus Phtēr*** — "Sky Father," bright daylight sky personified, supreme deity.
- ***H₂éusōs*** — the Dawn, his daughter, chased through the sky by primeval monsters.
- ***deiwós*** ("celestials") vs ***dhghmónio-*** ("terrestrials/earth-walkers") — the gods/humans split is encoded in the lexicon itself.
- "Middle space" (atmosphere) associated with second-function warriors.

**The signature formula — HERO SLAY SERPENT:**
- Serpent = watery-deep monster, "milk-suckler reptile," three-headed six-eyed cattle-thief; embodiment of Chaos/blockage.
- Hero = often Storm God (Indra smashing Vrtra; Thor fishing the world-serpent), wielding thunder-weapon (mace/hammer).
- Verb root: **\*gʷʰen-** ("to smite, slay") — rare, distinctive; its recurrence across branches is a diagnostic.
- Speculative origin note: "Cargo Cult of the Dnieper Rapids" — ritual response to neighboring agriculturalists' material culture.

**Tripartite social ideology:** priests (sovereignty) / warriors (force) / cultivators (fertility-prosperity).

**The koryos (first pass):** "itinerant werewolf society" of landless bachelors; "ritually dead" to families; battle-frenzy *furor/lyssa* (wolf-madness).

**Afterlife:** soul crosses a river of memory/forgetfulness to reach the "Broad One" (***Wélunos***) in the northern night sky. Underworld nexus: **World Tree + sacred well + prophetic severed head**.

**The Poet / Remembrancer:** professional of the spoken word; social broker between patrons and clients; uses "truth formulations" — precisely crafted speech believed to produce immediate physical effects. Words "woven as on a loom."

**Recurring ideological payload:** *"unfailing fame"* (***ḱléwos n̥-dʰgʷʰi-tom***) — the one thing valued above life. (Formula appears with identical etymological material in Homeric Greek + Vedic Sanskrit.)

## PART 2 — The Language Itself (Comparative Reconstruction)

**Nature of the evidence:** PIE is unattested — a "starred shadow" reconstructed from daughter-language correspondences via the comparative method ("technique of equations").

**Phonology:** The **laryngeals h₁, h₂, h₃** — purely hypothetical until Hittite was deciphered (early 20th c.) and showed actual h-sounds where theory predicted them. The great vindication of reconstruction.

**Anchor lexicon (dates the community):** horse ***h₁éḱ'wos***; wheel ***kʷékʷlos***; copper ***h₂áies-*** → Copper Age (4th millennium BC). Religion embedded: *Dyēus Phtēr*, *deiwos* from root "bright sky."

**Grammar:**
- Three genders (masc/fem/neut), evolved from earlier common/neuter two-gender system; feminine grammaticalized gradually.
- Verbs organized by **grammatical aspect**, not tense — whether an action is internally complex, bounded, or resultant-state, rather than when it happened. Two active conjugation sets: **m-conjugation** and **h₂e-conjugation** (the latter → the resultative-stative "perfect" stem in daughter branches).
- Registers: "language of gods" vs "language of men" — aesthetic markedness distinguishing sacred from mundane.

**Key concepts:**
- **"Truth formulation"** — speech with physical-world efficacy.
- **"Genetic intertextuality"** — identical poetic formulas across branches separated by thousands of miles (see: unfailing fame).
- Social structure: **patrilineal, patrilocal, exogamous**, imposed on encountered peoples.
- Expansion: 4th-millennium BC, possibly via "expansion cycles" linked to Cucuteni-Tripolye competition; reached "end regions" (Scandinavia, Ireland). Methodological note: "archaeopoetics" — *names less important than concepts and functions*.

## PART 3 — Koryos vs. Kouretes (The Two Youth-Bands)

The essential juxtaposition, both rooted in ***kor-*** ("young men/lads"):

| | **Koryos** | **Kouretes** |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Itinerant wolf-warband | Ritual shield-dancers |
| Domain | Wilds/frontier ("savagery") | Sanctuary/center ("culture") |
| Noise | Terror — howls of raiding | Protective din — blade-on-shield to hide infant Zeus |
| Hair | Unshorn locks, wild | Shorn front / long back, dedicated to rivers at adulthood |
| Possession | Beast-spirit (*lyssa*) | Liturgical spirit |
| Function | Plunder until marriageable status | Guard the divine infant; support sovereign |

**Key structural point:** both are ***kouroi*** — liminal youths between mother's house and householder status. The Kouretes are plausibly the **ritualized memory** of the koryos (cf. Romulus's band seizing the Sabine women; the Roman *Salii* "leaping priests" in bronze kilts). Not a linear evolution — "history is a muddy flood, not a straight road."

**The koryos lifecycle (Part 4 elaborates):**
1. Landless sons, "é-cland"/clanless — mandatory rite of passage.
2. Winter-solstice dog sacrifice (site: **Krasnosamarskoe**) — "the boy dies, the warrior is born."
3. Wolfskins or nakedness = deliberate move culture→nature.
4. Battle-frenzy: *furor/menos/lyssa* (Greek), *berserksgangr* (Norse). Speed, agility, howling.
5. Recession circa age ~19+: beard grown, loot won, **cleansing ritual** (baptism/vat immersion).
6. Collective marriage; entry into the ***tewt*** (tribe) as soldier-farmers/householders.

Led by senior males teaching war, trickery, and truth-formulations. Vedic cognate: the ***Vrātyas*** — sworn brotherhoods with winter sacrifices, poetry recitation, chariot races. Reflexes across Baltic, Slavic, Germanic, Greek, Roman, Anatolian. Herodotus's Neuri: whole tribe becomes wolves annually (as legend). **Function:** the expansionist engine of IE spread.

## PART 6 — Emily Lyle, "A Structure for the Gods: The Indo-European Pantheon Reconsidered"

The scholarly keystone. Core thesis and mechanics:

**Thesis:** A coherent tenfold pantheon plausibly existed in prehistory, mapped onto a spatio-temporal-social structure. It decayed in the oral period; writing fragmented it further. ("Rather difficult to convey a total cosmological scheme in writing.")

**Building from Dumézil but beyond him:** Dumézil's three functions hold up; his specific god-placements (via the Mitanni treaty and Adam of Bremen's Uppsala account) are rejected as misinterpretations. Allen's insight: the structures are remnants of a **primitive classification system**.

**The ten-position scheme (the "Edinburgh reference set"):**
- 4 directions/spatial positions + a possible 5th center, cross-cut with light/dark doubling → 10 positions.
- Center: the **trifunctional king figures** (positions 5–6).
- The eight outer gods defined by **three binary oppositions**:
  - **A: hot/cold** (above/below; ~above=hot)
  - **B: dry/wet**
  - **C: light/dark** (the outer gods are all the *dark* term: the "old gods")
- The **four "old gods"** = the four elements as cosmic layers: **air, fire, earth, water** (cf. Aegir/sea, Hræsvelgr/wind, Surt/fire, and earth via Gymir/Jörd in Norse).
- The "young gods" succeed the old in time.

**Temporal mapping (via Tolstoy):** the year-cycle corresponds to the 24-hour day cycle; the **twelve days around midwinter** (and the last-light-to-first-light night period) are the dark/dangerous/death-reversal period dedicated to the **old gods**; the rest of the year belongs to the young gods.

**Age-grade speculation:** in prehistory, force→young men, fertility→mature men, sacred→old men.

**Methodological warning (her own):** don't reify named gods — a name attaches to cult-place/story, not to fixed character. **Functions and positions precede names.**
