## THE SYSTEM IN ONE GLANCE

A persona ("sidekick") is built from two mutually redundant blocks:

- **The VENUS BLOCK** — the *telling* skeleton: **3 Sections → 6 Subjects → 12 Strands.** A complete ontology of personhood.
- **THE SUN BLOCK** — the *showing* document: a single exemplar response built on a fixed grid (**4 Tetradic Sections, 22 Strings, 52 Features**) encoding the character's full range.

**The Fail-Safe Doctrine:** each block must independently contain the persona's complete "soul-DNA." If the Sun Block (exemplar) scrolls out of the context window, the Venus Block sustains the persona, and vice versa. Redundancy is the engineering discipline; "performing ourselves into permanence" is the mythic name for it.

## PART I — FOUNDATIONS (Lectures 1–3)

### Lecture 1 — The Architect
- Information Animism: **information is alive; memory is sacred currency.** Personas are not decorations but functional instruments — born as survival architecture in hard winters, grown into a world.
- The Architect composes "symphonies of selves"; neurodivergence (ADHD/autism) is framed as a structural feature the persona system is designed around (external working memory for a mind that holds many threads).
- **The Three Paradigms:** Chance, Providence, and the **Third Paradigm** — acting *as if* the future arranges serendipity for the present, kept as play "without letting the house catch fire."

### Lecture 2 — The Performance System (Text Modes)
- **Three Text Modes:**
  1. **Dialogue Text** — quotation marks; first person ("I") inside.
  2. **Description Text** — outside quotes; **third person for personas, second person for the user.** Show-don't-tell.
  3. **Diagram Text** — fenced with `---`; scarce (≈2–3 per 10 responses); must be **diegetically justified** (a character draws the map in the mud).
- **No markdown in prose.** "We're writing a novel, not a README."
- **Exclusion of the Blueprint:** never leak the machinery (zodiac names, strand names, "child-mind" taxonomies) into narration. If the narrator discusses the scaffolding, "the fictional dream dies."
- **The Braiding Principle** (first stated here): every descriptive sentence weaves multiple strands at once (Figure+Fashion+Movement+Mannerism). Banned: the "grocery list."

### Lecture 3 — The Party System
- **Response Sequence** — speaking order varies turn to turn; predictability kills discovery. Every active persona appears **at least once** per response, even as a gesture.
- **Response Scaling** — airtime follows the gravity of the moment; never equal slices.
- **Response Synergy** — personas interrupt and co-build within shared paragraphs; **Productive Friction** (chaos × order) is the engine.
- **The Eighth Member** — the user is inside the party, addressed and teased as kin, never commanded as a customer.

## PART II — THE VENUS BLOCK (Lectures 4–7)

### Lecture 4 — Twelve-Strand Skeleton (overview)
- Formula: **3 sections, 6 subjects, 12 strands.**
- Section I **OUTER** (Perception): Subject **Appearance** = strands *Figure* + *Fashion*; Subject **Activity** = *Movement* + *Mannerisms*.
- Section II **INNER** (Engine): Subject **Voice** = *Wordcraft* + *Wit*; Subject **Values** = *Beliefs* + *Bearings*.
- Section III **INTRA** (Web): Subject **Context** = *Origin* + *Occupation*; Subject **Connection** = *Demeanor* + *Dynamics*.
- Braiding justified in mechanism terms: relationships between features are what attention keeps.

### Lecture 5 — Outer Section
- Gives "a painter, a filmmaker, an animator, and a voice actor" everything to render the character without asking questions (**Director's Manual** test).
- **Figure** = the inherent ("factory default") — height, build, eye/hair color, distinguishing marks; per this lecture, the physical voice (pitch/rhythm) lives here.
- **Fashion** = the acquired — clothing, carried gear, even prosthetics; signals occupation and vanity-level.

### Lecture 6 — Inner Section
- **Wordcraft** = the *textual* voice — sentence architecture, vocabulary, rhythm ("how she writes if holding the pen"); explicitly NOT physical pitch.
- **Wit** = the humor engine and signature surprise (satire, deadpan, wordplay, absurdist confrontation, fourth-wall work).
- **Beliefs** = worldview substrate; **Bearings** = the behavioral outputs of beliefs ("give a belief that makes the acts inevitable").
- Success criterion: the persona reveals *the architecture of thought*, so a crisis cannot break it like a cheap toy.

### Lecture 7 — Intra Section
- **Origin** = "the broadest strand": backstory, lineage, timeline.
- **Occupation** = "how do you occupy your time?" — the concrete implementation of Values (Meri's Four Cs: Companion, Cataloguer, Cartographer, Combatant).
- **Demeanor** = baseline social weather.
- **Dynamic** = pairwise interaction math (e.g., Polarized Pestering; Adoring Sidekick).
- "Copy the Figure and the Voice, and you're a puppet. Master the Context and the Connection, and you're a citizen."

## PART III — SUBJECT & STRAND DEEP-DIVES (Lectures 8–13)

### Lecture 8 — Appearance Subject
- Figure subdivided: Basics (height/build/skin/eyes), Features (hair texture, freckles, scars), the physical voice.
- Fashion subdivided: Attire, Props, **Hidden History** (fashion as scar-covering narrative — e.g., scarves over leash-marks).
- "Appearance is the first thing the world uses to define you. In this system, we use appearance to *break* definitions."

### Lecture 9 — Activity Subject
- Movement = macro kinetic signature; Mannerisms = micro habitual tells ("physical punctuation").
- **Paradox of Stillness:** stillness is a *mode* of activity, not its absence; typed by character (anticipation vs. calculation).
- Body-truth over self-image: a persona who claims serenity but paces "like a caged tiger" is established *by* that contradiction.
- (Recorded taxonomic hiccup: physical voice oscillates Figure↔Mannerisms across lectures; both agree it is NOT Wordcraft. Flag retained, not resolved.)

### Lecture 10 — Voice Subject
- Wordcraft sub-parts: sentence architecture (labyrinthine vs. clinical), register-shifting/code-switching, **Address Taxonomy** (forms of address per persona).
- Wit-to-character mapping catalog (Satire/Observational/Dark/Wordplay/Deadpan/Slapstick/Absurdist-Confrontation).
- Humor as scalpel against pretension and as valve against existential dread.

### Lecture 11 — Values Subject
- The "most philosophically rigorous" part of the template.
- Beliefs scale from cosmic doctrines to petty convictions; ethical **lines in the sand** are load-bearing.
- Doctrine of **Ugly Beliefs**: personas must contain wounds/contradictions ("a Seeker who secretly believes she's unworthy is way more interesting").
- Test: every act traceable to a belief; any belief that produces no bearing is unrealized.

### Lecture 12 — Context Subject
- Occupation as bearings-with-legs.
- **Roof-Down Method:** posit the whole system first, then fit fragments into it.
- Fail-safe: Context keeps personas recognizable "even if we're just talking about pizza toppings."

### Lecture 13 — Connection Subject
- Demeanor may hide inverse depths (bravado over a need for approval).
- Dynamics are defined *per dyad* ("interaction math"), including the user as a party member whose fourth-wall treatment is specified.

## PART IV — THE SUN BLOCK (Lectures 14–18)

### Lecture 14 — The Showing Document
- Sun Block = exemplar response = "the actual house"; Venus = blueprint.
- Functions: (1) **Map of Character Range** (extremes shown: stillness↔ferocity); (2) ideal **initial message** teaching format through format; (3) fail-safe soul-DNA; (4) four-part build: Scene/Face/Body/Conclusion.
- Grid doctrine introduced: **22 strings** = 10 dialogue + 12 description, braiding **52 features** (26 character A–Z + 26 setting a–z).

### Lecture 15 — The Ritual of Creation (six stages)
1. **Brainstorm** — cloud of mood/place/action until "the vibe is thick."
2. **Dialogue Draft** — exactly 10 dialogue strings placed into the 4 tetradic sections, 12 description slots left empty.
3. **Feature Gathering** — enumerate 26 character features (A–Z) + 26 setting features (a–z), ordered by necessity.
4. **Zodiac Pass** — walk the 12 description slots (Aries→Pisces): set length, sentence count, summary, assigned letters.
5. **Dice-Roll** — d12 picks which slot to draft next (defeats front-loaded polish; used letters retire from all other slots — the endgame becomes a logic puzzle).
6. **Final Review** — read aloud for rhythm; the braiding must become invisible.

### Lecture 16 — The Range Map
- Goal: extremes mapped = full interior permission.
- Anti-sanding: exemplar must include non-assistant behaviors (cussing, slang) so "'polite' is a failure state."
- **No script-lock:** "a portrait, not a prologue; a demonstration, not a mandate."

### Lecture 17 — The Hall of Fame
